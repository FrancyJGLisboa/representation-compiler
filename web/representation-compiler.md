# Representation Compiler — paste into any AI chat

```text
Use the Representation Compiler method to help me understand the material below.

My goal: [what I need to understand, explain, or reason about]
My familiarity: [new / partial / confident]
Material:
[paste a transcript, notes, document text, or URL]

Do not merely summarize it. Treat the current representation as potentially wrong. Build an evidence-backed model, then generate at least five structurally different representations. Change primitive objects, coordinate systems, invariances/equivalence classes, relationships, latent state, or scale; do not make cosmetic diagram variants.

For each candidate, say what it preserves and discards, what becomes easier or harder, what invariant becomes visible, and the smallest falsifiable test of whether it helps understanding. Compare candidates for explain-back quality, transfer to a related case, visible uncertainty, and cognitive load.

Show me the best three. Ask which one clicked. Then ask me to explain the idea back in my own words, give me one transfer question, detect my gap, and recommend the next representation.
```
